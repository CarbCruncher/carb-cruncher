About Carb Cruncher

Making everyday food choices a little easier.

Carb Cruncher was created with a simple goal: make carbohydrate information easier to find, understand, and use in everyday life.

Created by a registered nurse, Carb Cruncher was inspired by the realization that finding reliable, easy-to-understand food information shouldn't be complicated, intimidating, or expensive.

For many people, managing meals and understanding carbohydrates can feel overwhelming. Nutrition information is often presented in ways that are difficult to understand, especially for people who are new to carbohydrate awareness, older adults, busy families, or anyone who simply wants a straightforward answer.

We believe everyone deserves access to information that helps them make informed food choices.

More than a food lookup tool

Carb Cruncher is designed to make everyday meal planning easier.

  • Look up foods: Find carbohydrate information for everyday foods and ingredients.
  • Build a Meal: Add foods together and see your meal's carbohydrate total.
  • Cook as You Go: Add ingredients as you prepare a meal.
  • Save Recipes: Keep your favorite meals and customize ingredients.
  • Explore Meal Ideas: Find inspiration for everyday cooking.

Our Philosophy

Food information should be simple, approachable, and accessible to everyone.

We believe technology should make everyday life easier, not create another barrier. That's why our goal is to keep Carb Cruncher affordable, easy to navigate, and welcoming to people of all ages and levels of technology experience.

No complicated nutrition language. No judgment. Just useful information to help you make more informed choices.

A Note About Nutrition Information

Carb Cruncher is an educational tool intended to provide general food and carbohydrate information. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, or replace individualized medical or nutritional advice. Always consult your healthcare professional for guidance specific to your needs.
